Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the most common questions I receive as a freelance designer. If you don’t see your question here, feel free to reach out—I’m happy to help!

1.

What services do you offer?

I specialize in end-to-end digital solutions spanning UI/UX design, custom software development, and strategic digital ventures. Through Atomiq Agency and my independent work, I deliver wireframing, web and mobile app design, library intelligence platforms (Libblio), and technology solutions for community organizations.

2.

How does the design process work?

My process begins by uncovering the core problem and user needs. From there, I move through rapid wireframing, iterative interactive prototyping, usability testing, and high-fidelity interface design. Every step is built on close collaboration, feedback loops, and clarity.

3.

How long does a project usually take?

Project timelines vary with scope and goals. Design sprints and rapid prototypes typically take 1 to 2 weeks, while comprehensive web and mobile apps, full-scale platforms, or brand identity systems usually range from 4 to 8 weeks with clearly defined milestones.

4.

What do I need to provide before starting a project?

An overview of your goals, target audience, core deliverables, and any existing brand assets, wireframes, or reference materials. If you are starting from an early concept, we can define the scope and requirements together from day one.

5.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Structured revision cycles are included at each milestone—from early wireframes to final polishing. We test and review together so the finished product aligns precisely with your expectations and quality standards.

6.

How do I get started?

You can send a message through the contact form below, email me directly at hello@axelkarambizi.com, or reach out on WhatsApp at +250 788 749 709. Let's discuss your project and build something remarkable.
